# Tiny Transit

Tiny Transit is a minimalist transit management game about drawing lines, moving passengers, and holding a growing city together for as long as you can.

It is built as a loving tribute to the original modern transit classic that inspired so many players to think about maps, movement, and urban flow in a completely different way. Tiny Transit does not aim to replace that experience. It is a small, affectionate homage to its clarity, tension, elegance, and rhythm.

This project is a complete clean-room reimagining built from the ground up. No original code, art, audio, or game assets were used in its production. Every visual element, interaction, and line of gameplay logic has been created independently to make Tiny Transit a modern, lightweight, and open-source tribute in its own right.

## What the game is about

You connect stations, shape routes, expand your network, and respond to the city as it grows. What begins as a calm, readable system gradually turns into a balancing act between capacity, geography, timing, and pressure.

The challenge is not just to build lines, but to build the right lines at the right moment:

- extend the network without creating bottlenecks
- keep stations from overcrowding
- decide when to invest in new trains, extra carriages, bridges, and new lines
- absorb sudden surges from airports and ports
- adapt as the map expands and the city becomes harder to manage

## The feel of Tiny Transit

Tiny Transit is designed around a clean visual language and a fast, readable play loop. The goal is to keep the board easy to understand even as the network becomes dense and complicated.

The game leans into:

- simple geometric stations
- elegant route drawing
- expanding terrain with coastlines, inland water, airports, and ports
- rising economic pressure through scaling item prices
- score-chasing and survival tension

It is meant to feel thoughtful, tense, and satisfying in short sessions, while still giving room for improvisation and personal style.

## A tribute

Tiny Transit exists because the original game left a lasting mark. Its design showed how much beauty and drama could live inside a stripped-down transport simulation.

This project is a tribute to that spirit:

- the joy of sketching a network into existence
- the quiet panic of an overloaded station
- the satisfaction of a line finally clicking into place
- the strange beauty of a city seen as flow, timing, and shape

If you know the classic, you will feel the inspiration. If you do not, Tiny Transit still stands as its own compact city-management challenge.

## Current features

- route building and reshaping
- closed loops and line extensions
- trains, extra engines, and extra carriages
- bridges for inland water crossings
- dynamic terrain with optional coastlines, rivers, and lakes
- airports and ports that create major passenger surges
- day and night cycle
- score, revenue, and survival pressure
- online leaderboard support
- original in-game music and a music toggle

## Project spirit

Tiny Transit is intended to be approachable, readable, and alive. It is a small game about systems under pressure, but also about finding order inside complexity.

At its best, it creates the feeling that every new stop, every new connection, and every small decision matters.
